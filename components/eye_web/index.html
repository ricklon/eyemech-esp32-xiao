Lids

The two below are the character of the face, not a tweak: how hard each lid pair follows vertical gaze. 0.80 upper and 0.40 lower is the reference. Record a change you decide to keep in docs/decisions.md.

Jog — relative steps, for establishing direction

After any mechanical change, take ONE step and watch which way the linkage goes before jogging further. A lid rests against its closed stop, so the closing direction has no headroom — and these servos have the torque to break a printed part rather than stall.

Servos

Calibration mode: angle fields write directly to the servo. Small steps.

Min µs and Max µs are the pulse range the servo is driven with, not a limit — changing them changes what every angle means, including the ones already measured. A servo that only honours 1000–2000 µs under-travels silently, which shows up as lids that never fully close.
